The Problem We're Solving

Every day, incense smoke in Bhutanese monasteries and homes silently harms health and damages irreplaceable cultural treasures.

  • Incense is the most common indoor air pollutant in Bhutan
  • Temple air tests show dust levels way past safety limits
  • Greasy soot permanently blackens sacred artwork
  • Current filters clog quickly and are expensive to replace

The Innovation

Electrostatic Technology

80-90% Efficiency
